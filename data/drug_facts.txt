DRUG FACTS — adult oral use

TYLENOL REGULAR STRENGTH  (acetaminophen 325 mg per tablet)
  Each tablet contains 325 mg acetaminophen. The product relieves minor pain and fever.
  Adults and children 12 years and over: take 2 tablets (650 mg) per dose, at 4-to-6 hour
  intervals while symptoms persist. Do not take more than 10 tablets (3,250 mg) in 24 hours
  unless a doctor directs otherwise. Use beyond 10 days requires medical direction.
  Warnings: excessive acetaminophen causes liver damage. Risk rises with duplicate
  acetaminophen-containing medicines and with three or more alcoholic drinks daily during use.
  Avoid concurrent acetaminophen-containing medicines and use with a known ingredient allergy.
  Liver disease requires consultation before use. Warfarin use requires advice from a doctor
  or pharmacist. Skin reactions require stopping use and prompt medical help. Worsening or
  prolonged pain, fever lasting over three days, new symptoms, redness, or swelling require
  medical advice. Pregnancy or breastfeeding requires professional advice. Suspected overdose
  requires immediate medical help, even without symptoms.

TYLENOL EXTRA STRENGTH  (acetaminophen 500 mg per caplet)
  Each caplet contains 500 mg acetaminophen.
  Adults and children 12 years and over: take 2 caplets (1,000 mg) per dose, at 6-hour
  intervals while symptoms persist. Do not take more than 6 caplets in 24 hours unless a
  doctor directs otherwise. Six caplets contain 3,000 mg. Use beyond 10 days requires medical
  direction.
  Warnings: the liver warning identifies intake above 4,000 mg in 24 hours, concurrent
  acetaminophen medicines, and three or more alcoholic drinks daily as risks. That warning
  does not replace the six-caplet product limit. Avoid other acetaminophen medicines and known
  ingredient allergies. Seek advice before use with liver disease or warfarin. Skin reactions
  require stopping use and prompt help. Worsening or prolonged pain, fever over three days,
  new symptoms, redness, or swelling require medical advice. Pregnancy or breastfeeding
  requires professional advice. Suspected overdose requires immediate medical help even
  without symptoms.

ADVIL  (ibuprofen 200 mg per tablet)
  Each tablet contains 200 mg ibuprofen, an NSAID used for minor pain and fever.
  Adults and children 12 years and over: start with 1 tablet (200 mg) every 4 to 6 hours while
  symptoms persist; 2 tablets (400 mg) may be used if 1 tablet is ineffective. Use the
  smallest effective dose and take no more than 6 tablets (1,200 mg) per 24 hours unless a
  doctor directs otherwise.
  Warnings: severe allergy, gastrointestinal bleeding, and cardiovascular events. Bleeding risk
  increases with prior ulcers, anticoagulants, steroids, other NSAIDs, older age, alcohol, or
  excessive use. Avoid use following a previous allergic reaction to pain relievers and around
  heart surgery. Seek advice for stomach problems, relevant cardiovascular, liver or kidney
  disease, asthma, diuretics, serious conditions, or other medicines. Stop and seek help for
  allergy, bleeding signs, cardiovascular symptoms, worsening symptoms, pain beyond ten days,
  or fever beyond three days. Pregnancy or breastfeeding requires advice; use at 20 weeks of
  pregnancy or later requires explicit medical direction. Suspected overdose requires immediate
  help.

GENERAL ACETAMINOPHEN CONTEXT
  FDA describes 4,000 mg per day as the maximum recommended adult acetaminophen total across
  all medicines. Product directions still apply, including lower product-specific limits.
